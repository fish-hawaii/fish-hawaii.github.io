About This Guide

This site presents the Hawaii Division of Aquatic Resources (DAR) fishing regulations in an easy-to-read format. All content is derived from the official fishing_regs_May_2025.pdf rulebook. For the authoritative source, always refer to the DAR website or visit a DAR office.
